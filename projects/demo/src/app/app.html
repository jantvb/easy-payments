Product

Editable in every mode. In Real / Test Providers the NestJS catalog seeds these fields (premium-plan → $99.00 USD) and the amount you set here is what the backend charges for Stripe flows (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay). PayPal, Klarna and Affirm always use the catalog price, since the library never sends them a browser amount. Affirm also requires a checkout total of at least ~$35 before its tile appears.
